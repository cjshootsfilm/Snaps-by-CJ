About

Snaps by CJ

I’m CJ, a photographer based in Devon, UK. I photograph places, light and everyday moments using 35mm film cameras, paper cameras and iPhone.

I use film stocks including Fujifilm 200, Kodak Gold, Kodak Ultramax 400 and Ilford HP5, along with others as I experiment.
